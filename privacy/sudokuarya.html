2. Data Collection & Processing Mechanics

We strictly minimize data collection to what is essential for application functionality:

A. Local On-Device Storage (Default)

All core gameplay data, including difficulty levels unlocked, active game states, solution histories, hint usage, revival keys, custom color themes, and ambient soundscape settings, are persisted locally on your device using an encrypted Room SQLite database. This data remains on your physical device unless you voluntarily activate Cloud Sync.

B. Physical Paper Sudoku Camera Scanner (Hardware Access)

Sudoku Arya features a paper puzzle optical camera scanner:

  • RAM-Only Computer Vision: Camera frames are analyzed in real-time volatile RAM purely to recognize numbers on paper puzzles.
  • No Images Stored or Sent: Camera images and video feeds are never recorded, stored, uploaded, or transmitted to any external server or third party.

C. Voluntary Cloud Synchronization (Google Firebase)

You may voluntarily choose to sign in with your Google account to synchronize progress across multiple Android devices:

  • Synchronized Data: User account ID, unlocked difficulty tiers, total games completed, best time scores, streak counts, and revival keys.
  • Encryption & Isolation: All cloud data is encrypted in transit via SSL/TLS 1.3 and stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under strict per-user security rules preventing unauthorized access.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure & Advertising

Sudoku Arya integrates industry-leading, compliant SDKs provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services & Credential Manager: Used for secure authentication, app integrity verification, and Play Store updates.
  • Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: Used exclusively for voluntary account sign-in and cloud game progress backup.
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Used to present contextual advertisements. AdMob may process device advertising identifiers (AD_ID) and diagnostic data strictly in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies and applicable privacy laws.

4. Google Play Zero-Permission Media Policy

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, Sudoku Arya does NOT request broad external media storage permissions (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`). Media interactions use Google's native, zero-permission Android Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA, GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Sudoku Arya complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). The app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. User Control, Rights & Data Deletion Protocol

You hold full control over your data and retain the following rights:

  • In-App Data Purge: You can wipe all local progress, stats, and cached records at any time by selecting Settings > Sign Out & Clear Local Data.
  • Cloud Account Deletion Request: To request permanent deletion of your cloud account and all associated Firestore records, email support@amrton.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All associated cloud records will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Security Safeguards

We employ robust technical controls to safeguard your data, including network security configs blocking cleartext HTTP traffic, ProGuard/R8 obfuscation stripping debug logs from release builds, and zero-trust Firestore security rules.

8. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect app features or legal compliance standards. Updates will be posted directly to apps.amrton.com/privacy/sudokuarya.html and within the application.
