Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Infinity MultiView Browser, developed, distributed, and maintained by Amrton ("we", "us", or "our"). We believe strongly in transparency, minimal data collection, and user autonomy.

Core Principle: Amrton does NOT operate remote user tracking servers, sell your browsing records, or log your personal web activities. Your browsing history, favorites, and settings exist strictly on your local device.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Infinity MultiView Browser mobile application available on the Google Play Store and explains what data is processed, how local storage operates, what third-party SDKs are integrated, and how your privacy is safeguarded.

02

Information We Do Not Collect

Amrton does not collect, transmit, or harvest personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No User Account: The application does not require user registration, email sign-ups, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Server-Side Tracking: We do not log what websites you visit, the URLs you type, or the content of the pages you view to any external servers.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request, access, or track your GPS location coordinates.
  • No Microphone / Camera / Contact Access: We do not request or access your private photos, contacts, camera, or microphone.
03

Local On-Device Data Storage

To deliver a seamless multi-view productivity experience, the application stores certain functional preferences locally on your device using Android's private app sandbox (SQLite Room Database and Encrypted SharedPreferences):

Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Browsing History Allows you to review and reopen previously launched multi-screen URLs. Local Room Database (On-device sandbox only)
Saved Bookmarks Stores your favorite shortcut websites for one-tap launching. Local Room Database (On-device sandbox only)
User Preferences Saves selected screen layout (1, 2, 4, 6, 8 screens), auto-reload timer interval, desktop/mobile user agent choice, and theme (Light/Dark). Local SharedPreferences (On-device sandbox only)

None of this local data is ever transmitted to Amrton or third parties. It remains stored exclusively in your device's isolated application storage.

04

WebKit & Android System WebView Data

Each browser tile in Infinity MultiView Browser runs using Android's official system-level WebKit / WebView component.

When you browse websites:

  • Cookies, session storage, and web cache are managed directly by Android's WebKit engine.
  • These temporary web assets are isolated within your app container.
  • You can clear all web cookies, cache, and history at any time using the in-app "Clear History" button or through Android System Settings > Apps > Infinity MultiView Browser > Clear Storage.
05

Foreground Services & Background Execution

Infinity MultiView Browser includes an optional Background Execution Mode designed for users who want multi-screen audio, web streams, or background refresh loops to stay alive when switching between apps or locking their screen.

  • Service Notification: In compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies, whenever the background execution mode is activated, a persistent foreground notification is displayed in the Android notification drawer.
  • Transparency: This notification clearly informs you that a multi-view session is running in the background and provides an instant one-tap control to stop the service.
  • No Secret Background Processing: The foreground service executes exclusively while you have granted permission and terminates immediately when stopped or when the app process is closed.

07

Android System Permissions

Infinity MultiView Browser adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege, requesting only permissions essential to its core functions:

Permission Classification Explicit Purpose
INTERNET Normal (Install-time) Required to load web pages in browser tiles and fetch advertisements.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Monitors whether the device is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular data before initiating page reloads.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Normal (Install-time) Allows user-requested background playback and multi-screen session persistence.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Required by Android OS to show the background session status notification.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Optional User Prompt Prevents Android OS from prematurely terminating active background reload loops.
08

Data Retention & Deletion Policy

You have complete control over any local data stored by the application:

  • In-App Clear History: Opening the History tab and tapping the "Clear All History" button immediately purges all saved URL session entries from your device's local database.
  • Complete Deletion on Uninstall: Uninstalling the application automatically and permanently deletes all associated databases, bookmarks, caches, and SharedPreferences files from your device.
  • No Cloud Remnants: Since we do not transmit or store your data on external cloud servers, there are zero remote copies to purge.
09

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Infinity MultiView Browser is a general utility productivity tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 for residents of the European Economic Area).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the browser without supervision, you can easily remove all local records by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app. If you have any inquiries, please contact us at amrtonofficial@gmail.com.

GDPR & CCPA / CPRA Disclosures

For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to know what data is collected. All data processed by Amrton is visible locally within your app screens.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can exercise your right to erasure at any instant by using the in-app deletion button or clearing app storage.
  • No "Sale" of Personal Data: Amrton does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or browsing records to third parties for monetary or valuable consideration.
  • Opt-Out of Targeted Advertising: You can opt out of interest-based ads via your Android system advertising controls.
11

Data Security Practices

We implement industry-standard mobile security architectures:

  • Android Sandbox Architecture: All local database and configuration files are stored inside your device's protected internal storage space, inaccessible to other unprivileged applications.
  • Encrypted Transport: All web requests initiated by the browser prioritize modern HTTPS transport protocols with end-to-end TLS encryption provided by the visited websites.
  • R8 Code Obfuscation & Shrinking: The application release binary is compiled and hardened using Google R8 full-mode optimization to prevent reverse-engineering and tampering.
