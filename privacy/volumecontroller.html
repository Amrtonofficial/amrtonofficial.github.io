Zero Personal Data Collection Architecture

Volume Controller is engineered with a strict local-first philosophy. We do not operate external user data ingestion servers, do not maintain user databases, do not track behavioral telemetry across applications, and do not access your microphone or personal files. Your audio controls remain 100% private and sandboxed within your Android device.

1 Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Volume Controller ("the Application", "we", "our"), developed and maintained by Amrton ("the Developer", "Company"), handles device interactions, permissions, and data privacy.

Volume Controller is an assistive accessibility utility designed to provide intuitive, tactile on-screen controls for Android audio streams (Media, Ringtone, Notifications, Alarms, Voice Calls, and System Sounds). It offers users a reliable software solution for damaged or broken physical hardware volume buttons, as well as customizable floating controls and status notification tiles.

This policy applies to all releases of the application distributed via the Google Play Store, direct APK distributions, and associated services provided by Amrton.

2 Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

Amrton upholds the highest standards of user confidentiality. We explicitly confirm that Volume Controller does NOT collect, harvest, transmit, store, sell, or monetize any personal information.

  • Zero Audio Recording or Listening: The application does NOT request the RECORD_AUDIO permission and never accesses the device microphone. Volume adjustments are strictly numerical commands routed to the Android OS AudioManager.
  • No Personal Identity Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
  • No Contact or Calendar Access: The app does not access your address book, call logs, SMS messages, or social contacts.
  • No Location Telemetry: We do not track, query, or log coarse (Cell/Wi-Fi) or fine (GPS) geographic location.
  • No File or Photo Scanning: The app has zero access to external storage files, photo libraries, or media collections.
  • No Cross-App Tracking or Ad Profiling: We do not request the Android Advertising Identifier (AD_ID) and do not build marketing profiles.

3 Android Permissions & Purpose Specification

In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, every permission requested by Volume Controller is strictly necessary to deliver its core assistive functionality. Below is the full specification of permissions declared in the application manifest:

Display Over Other Apps SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

Allows the optional assistive floating ball and quick volume slider dialog to overlay on top of other running applications, games, or fullscreen video players, enabling instant volume control without pressing physical keys.

Foreground Service & Special Use FOREGROUND_SERVICE & SPECIAL_USE

Enables the persistent background volume controller notification tile and floating assistive overlay to remain active and responsive without being terminated by Android battery management systems.

Do Not Disturb Policy Access ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY

Required on Android 7.0+ (API 24+) solely if the user elects to switch Ringtone and Notification streams into complete Silent or Vibrate modes without triggering Android system security blocks.

Post Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) to display the interactive media slider tile in the system notification shade for rapid volume adjustments from the lockscreen or notification drawer.

Modify Audio Settings MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

Standard Android audio API capability enabling the application to increment, decrement, and query stream volume levels via the official AudioManager.

Haptic Vibration Feedback VIBRATE

Provides subtle haptic feedback when dragging sliders or tapping volume buttons to confirm touch interaction.

Restore on Device Restart RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Automatically restores your chosen floating button and notification tile configurations after the device restarts, only if enabled in user preferences.

4 On-Device Data Storage & Persistence

Volume Controller retains only the visual preferences and functional configurations you explicitly choose within the application settings:

  • Floating ball coordinates, size, and transparency levels
  • Selected audio stream sliders (Media, Ring, Call, Notification, Alarm, System)
  • Theme selection (Dark Mode, Light Mode, Dynamic System Material 3)
  • Boot autostart toggle preferences

Storage Mechanism: All user configurations are saved exclusively on your local device via encrypted Android SharedPreferences and Jetpack Room database storage. This data is physically sandboxed by the Android kernel. It is never transmitted across the network, and uninstalling the application permanently deletes all stored configurations.

Google AdMob may process standardized Android advertising identifiers and technical diagnostic signals in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies to serve ads, frequency-cap impressions, and prevent fraud. Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android device Settings > Google > Ads.

No audio, personal identifiers, or IP addresses are linked to these diagnostic logs.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Volume Controller is a general utility designed for all age demographics. We do not solicit, target, or knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Economic Area).

Because the Application collects zero personal information, it complies fully with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), European GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy Requirements.

8 Security Architecture

The security of your device and configuration integrity is a paramount priority:

  • OS Sandboxing: Volume Controller runs inside the dedicated security sandbox provided by the Linux kernel underlying Android. Other third-party apps cannot access its local preference files.
  • Cryptographic Release Signing: Every release binary is cryptographically compiled and signed using the official Amrton release keystore, preventing unauthorized modification or tampering.
  • R8 Code Obfuscation: Production builds undergo rigorous R8 dead-code shrinking and identifier obfuscation to safeguard against reverse-engineering and security vulnerabilities.
