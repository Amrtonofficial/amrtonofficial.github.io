Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

Application: AI Pixel Repair

Developer: Amrton

1. Overview

AI Pixel Repair ("the App"), developed by Amrton, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and security when you use our screen diagnostic and pixel restoration application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

No Personal Data Collected: AI Pixel Repair does not require user accounts, logins, or personal registration. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, user identifiers, contact information, location data, or device identifiers.

3. Local Device Processing

All screen diagnostic sweeps, subpixel excitation patterns, calibration calculations, and session duration logs operate 100% locally on your device. No diagnostic data or screen metrics leave your phone.

4. Permissions

The App requests standard Android runtime flags strictly required for core functionality:

  • Keep Screen Awake: Prevents display timeout during active pixel repair cycles.
  • Screen Brightness Control: Boosts screen luminance to maximum during diagnostic runs for peak subpixel stimulation.
  • Vibration & Audio Alerts: Provides haptic/audio notifications upon session completion.

5. Health & Safety Disclaimer

The App generates high-frequency subpixel flashing and color sequences. Users should refrain from looking directly at the display during active restoration sessions to prevent visual fatigue, eye strain, or temporary blurry vision.
