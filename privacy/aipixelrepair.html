2. Zero Personal Data Tracking

AI Pixel Repair operates under a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not require account registration, social login, or personal profile creation. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of the following:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Location Data or GPS tracking coordinates.
  • Contact lists, SMS messages, or private media files.
  • Biometric data, sensor logs, or personal usage history.

AI Pixel Repair integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited) to serve banner, interstitial, and native advertisements. This enables us to offer screen calibration and diagnostic tools free of charge.

4. 100% On-Device Processing

All screen repair protocols (Subpixel Shock, Quantum Matrix Noise, Raster Wave Sweeping, Max Voltage Inversion, Harmonic Frequency Chirp, and Molecular Stabilization) execute entirely on-device using local GPU/CPU hardware rendering and local timer loops. No display telemetry or calibration data is uploaded to remote servers.

5. Android Runtime Permissions

The application requests standard Android system permissions strictly necessary for display excitation operations:

  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps the screen awake during active auto-stop repair cycles so the display panel stays illuminated.
  • VIBRATE: Triggers a completion haptic pulse when an automated repair timer finishes.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch AdMob advertisements and check network connectivity status.

6. Visual Safety & Health Disclaimer

Photosensitivity & Visual Fatigue Warning

AI Pixel Repair emits rapid subpixel chromatic flashing and high-frequency color transitions designed to excite stuck display subpixels. Users are advised not to look directly at the screen during an active repair session. Staring directly at high-frequency flashing patterns may cause visual fatigue, eye strain, or temporary blurry vision. Place your phone face down while repair is in progress.
