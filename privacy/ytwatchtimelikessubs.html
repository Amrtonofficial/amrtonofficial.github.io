1. Purpose of the Policy

This policy explains how personal, device, and security-signal data collected during the use of the YtWatchTime Likes Subs application is securely processed, stored, used, and, when necessary, shared in accordance with applicable global data privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).

By downloading, accessing, or continuing to use the YtWatchTime Likes Subs application, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

To provide a reliable community platform, manage points, and guarantee fair rewards, YtWatchTime Likes Subs processes the following categories of data:

2.1 Device & System Data

  • Device brand, model, manufacturer, and hardware specs
  • Android OS version, build fingerprint, and kernel tags
  • Hardware ID (HWID), Android ID, and network state
  • OpenGL renderer and graphics runtime information
  • App version, build numbers, and crash diagnostics

2.2 User Account Data

  • Google Sign-In email, display name, and avatar URL
  • YouTube channel name and associated public channel ID
  • Completed voluntary watch tasks, likes, and subscription proofs
  • Points/coins balance and activity logs

2.3 App Usage & Billing Data

  • Campaign creation history and watch duration metrics
  • In-app purchases processed via Google Play Billing
  • App session diagnostics and navigation events
  • Firebase Analytics metrics and push notification alerts

2.4 Security & Anti-Abuse Signals

  • VPN active flag, proxy network detection, transport type
  • Root binaries check (su, Magisk, busybox)
  • Emulator / virtual environment detection score
  • ADB debugging active flag and debuggable runtime check
  • Auto-clicker & macro accessibility risk flags
Data Minimization Notice: Only boolean flags, integrity scores, and cryptographic hash values are collected for anti-abuse checks. We never access your private chats, contacts, gallery, keystrokes, or Google/YouTube passwords.

2.5 Optional Data (With User Consent)

  • Push Notifications: Optional notification permission to inform you when campaigns complete or when daily rewards become available.

3. Purposes of Data Use

We process collected data strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Operating the voluntary creator discovery and reward distribution mechanism.
  • Preventing malicious bot farming, multiple unauthorized device clones, and auto-clickers.
  • Authenticating user credentials securely via Google Sign-In and Credential Manager.
  • Promoting user campaigns strictly within YouTube Community Guidelines.
  • Diagnosing application crashes, performance bottlenecks, and system bugs.
  • Processing point store transactions and subscriptions via Google Play Billing.

4. Third-Party Integrations

YtWatchTime Likes Subs integrates with verified industry-leading service providers. Each provider manages data in adherence to strict security standards:

Google Firebase (Auth & Firestore) Google Identity Services Google Play In-App Billing Google AdMob

Zero Selling: Amrton does not sell, lease, or monetize your personal identity to data brokers or third-party advertising exchanges.

5. Anti-Fraud, Multi-Account, & Fair Play Rules

To preserve community fairness and prevent automated abuse, the following actions are strictly prohibited:

  • Multi-Account Farming: Creating more than 3 accounts on the same physical device or shared network will trigger an automatic hardware restriction.
  • Emulators & Modded APKs: Running YtWatchTime Likes Subs inside virtual machines, cloud emulators, or tampered APKs is restricted.
  • VPN & Proxy Spoofing: Hiding location through VPN tunnels or data-center proxies to manipulate reward pools is prohibited.
  • Auto-Clickers & Bots: Utilizing background macro scripts, accessibility auto-tappers, or automated viewers results in permanent bans.
  • Automated Decisions: Security breaches detected by our integrity engine may restrict account access. Users have the legal right to request manual human review (see Section 10).

6. Data Storage & Encryption

All network communication with our servers is safeguarded via encrypted TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Databases are protected by multi-tier Firestore security rules with granular access controls. Regular automated audits run continuously to isolate anomalies and protect creator confidentiality.

7. YouTube & In-App WebView Disclosure

YtWatchTime Likes Subs provides an embedded Android WebView to facilitate voluntary video playback and verified task validation:

  • Independent Platform: YtWatchTime Likes Subs is an independent application published by Amrton and is not sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated with YouTube LLC or Google LLC.
  • Cookies & Sessions: Standard session cookies are maintained within the isolated WebView sandbox solely to enable continuous video stream playback.
  • No Credential Access: We strictly avoid accessing, recording, or storing any Google or YouTube account passwords. All interactions occur directly on official YouTube web endpoints.

8. Global Regulatory Compliance

8.1 International Data Transfers

Data may be processed across secure cloud servers located across different regions (e.g., Google Cloud/Firebase facilities). All transfers comply with standard contractual clauses ensuring equal protection.

8.2 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

YtWatchTime Likes Subs is designated for individuals aged 13 and above. We do not intentionally harvest data from children under 13.

8.3 Data Retention

Account records and points transactions are retained while your profile is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently wiped from our production databases within 30 days.

8.4 California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA) & GDPR

You have the legal right to request data access, correction, portable export, and permanent deletion at any time without discrimination.
