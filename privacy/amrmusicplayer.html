🔒 Zero Third-Party Tracking Guarantee: AMR Music Player is designed as an offline-first high-fidelity media player. We do not collect, harvest, monetize, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or device media files.

1. Introduction

Welcome to AMR Music Player, developed and published by Amrton ("we", "us", or "our"). We are deeply committed to respecting and safeguarding the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details our operational practices regarding data collection, system permissions, local storage utilization, and network usage when you use our Android application.

2. Information Collection and Device Permissions

AMR Music Player requests specific Android system permissions strictly necessary to deliver core audio playback, playlist management, and user customization.

A. Media & Audio Files Permission (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Core Requirement

  • Purpose: Required to scan, index, organize, and play locally stored audio tracks and display embedded album art on your device.
  • Data Handling: All media indexing and playback take place exclusively on your local device. Your music files, metadata, and audio content are never uploaded, backed up, or shared to any external server, cloud infrastructure, or third party.

B. System Settings Permission (WRITE_SETTINGS) Optional

  • Purpose: Requested only if you explicitly choose the "Set as Ringtone" feature within the app.
  • Data Handling: Used solely to update your device's default system ringtone upon your direct action. No other system parameters are modified.

C. Notification & Foreground Service Permissions (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / FOREGROUND_SERVICE)

  • Purpose: Enables background playback and displays the active media player widget with media controls in your notification shade and lock screen.
  • Data Handling: Local system control only. No telemetry or notification data is collected or transmitted.

D. Network Access (INTERNET)

  • Purpose: Utilized solely for optional online synchronized lyrics fetching (via open public APIs such as LRCLIB) and missing metadata retrieval.
  • Data Handling: Only generic non-personal search queries (containing track title and artist name) are sent to retrieve lyrics. No personal identifiers, advertising IDs, or telemetry data are sent during network requests.

3. Local Storage & Data Persistence

All user-generated app state—including custom playlists, favorite tracks, playback history, equalizer presets, home screen widget configurations, and visual themes—is saved exclusively inside your device's private sandbox storage (App Local Storage).

Uninstalling the application or clearing the application data through Android System Settings permanently removes all locally saved application preferences.

4. Analytics, Advertising & Third-Party Disclosure

  • No Advertising SDKs: AMR Music Player contains zero advertisements, banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, or ad tracking frameworks (such as AdMob or Meta Audience Network).
  • No Analytics Profiling: We do not integrate third-party telemetry, crash-reporting SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics).
  • No Data Selling or Monetization: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

5. User Rights & Data Controls

Because all application data remains stored on your local hardware, you retain full ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Permission Revocation: You can grant or revoke storage and system permissions at any time via Android Settings > Apps > AMR Music Player > Permissions.
  • Data Deletion: You can clear all app-created playlists, settings, and caches by selecting Clear Data / Clear Cache in system settings or utilizing the in-app reset controls.

6. Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR-K)

AMR Music Player does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Our application complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you have any concerns regarding children's privacy, please contact us immediately.

7. Data Security

We prioritize data security by adhering to strict security architecture principles. By storing user data locally and eliminating server-side transmission of media files, your personal information is protected against unauthorized online intercept, data breaches, and remote leaks.

8. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or application enhancements. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.
