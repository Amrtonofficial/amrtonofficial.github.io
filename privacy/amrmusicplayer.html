1. Introduction

Welcome to AMR Music Player, developed and published by Amrton. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, handles, and protects your information when you use our Android application.

2. Information Collection and Usage

AMR Music Player is designed primarily as an offline media player. We respect your personal data and privacy.

A. Device Media & Storage Permission (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

  • Purpose: To scan, display, and play audio files stored on your device's local storage.
  • Data Handling: All audio files, metadata, and album artwork remain stored locally on your device. We do not upload, backup, or transfer your audio files or personal media to any external servers or cloud services.

B. System Settings Permission (WRITE_SETTINGS)

  • Purpose: Requested only if you explicitly choose to use the "Set as Ringtone" feature within the app.
  • Data Handling: This permission is strictly used to update your device's system ringtone preference as requested. No setting modifications occur without your explicit user action.

C. Network Access (INTERNET)

  • Purpose: Used solely for optional online lyrics fetching (via public APIs such as LRCLIB) and metadata lookup.
  • Data Handling: Only non-personal queries containing track title and artist name are sent to retrieve lyrics. No personal information, user identifiers, or device telemetry are transmitted.

D. Local App Storage

  • Purpose: To persist user preferences, created playlists, favorite tracks, playback history, equalizer settings, and home screen widget preferences.
  • Data Handling: All user data is stored locally in the application's private storage on your device.

3. Personal Data & Third-Party Sharing

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Collection: We do not require account creation, registration, or submission of personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use AMR Music Player.
  • No Data Selling or Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics brokers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics / Ads Tracking: AMR Music Player does not include third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or profiling tools.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted here with a revised "Effective Date".
